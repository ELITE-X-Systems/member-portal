# ELITEX DECREES

**Created by the Prosecutor — Mr. Cosimo**

These decrees establish the rules and expectations for members of the ELITE X group chat and community.

---

## 1. Respect Everyone

Treat all members with kindness and respect.

Disagreements are allowed, but personal attacks, insults, harassment, bullying, and intentionally backstabbing or targeting other members are not permitted.

---

## 2. Respect and Follow Admin Decisions

Admins are responsible for keeping the community organized and comfortable for everyone.

Members are expected to cooperate with administrative decisions. If you have concerns or disagreements regarding an admin decision, raise them respectfully through private messages rather than creating unnecessary conflict within the group.

---

## 3. No Trauma Dumping

Please avoid posting heavy personal problems, traumatic experiences, or similarly sensitive matters within the group chat.

The group chat is not intended to be a space for extensive trauma dumping. Please be mindful of the comfort and boundaries of other members.

---

## 4. No Slurs or Excessive Profanity

Keep the group chat welcoming and comfortable for everyone.

Slurs, excessive profanity, discriminatory language, and deliberately offensive or hateful language are not allowed.

---

## 5. No NSFW or Inappropriate Topics

Do not send, share, or discuss sexually explicit, NSFW, or otherwise inappropriate content within the group chat.

Keep conversations appropriate for everyone, as minors are involved in the community.

---

## 6. No Religious or Political Topics

Avoid religious and political debates or discussions within the group chat.

These topics can easily lead to unnecessary arguments and conflict, so they should be kept outside of group discussions.

---

## 7. No Unauthorized Promotion

Do not advertise, promote, or repeatedly share other groups, servers, communities, or similar platforms without permission from the admins.

If you wish to promote something, ask an admin for permission first.

---

## 8. Spam Is Allowed Within Reason

Casual spam, reactions, memes, and active conversations are welcome and allowed.

However, please do not overdo it or excessively spam messages, particularly while announcements or important information are being posted.

Make sure important messages remain visible and accessible to everyone.

---

## 9. Avoid AI Use and Plagiarism

Members are expected to respect original work and the efforts of other creators.

Do not steal, plagiarize, trace, copy, or claim another person's work as your own.

The use of artificial intelligence in commissions, particularly for visual artwork, is not permitted. Any commission found to involve AI-generated visual work may be terminated.

Always respect the original creator and provide proper credit when using or referencing someone else's work.

---

# Enforcement

These decrees exist to maintain a respectful, comfortable, and enjoyable environment for everyone in the ELITE X community.

Admins reserve the right to address violations and take appropriate action when necessary.

If you believe a rule has been violated or have concerns about a particular situation, contact an admin privately rather than escalating the situation publicly.
